01 · what it does

A mouse, minus the mouse.

AirMouse reads your webcam and drives the real Windows cursor — every app sees ordinary mouse input. Click, drag, right-click, scroll, volume, forward/back, minimise, and app launching are all hand gestures. A magnetic cursor sticks lightly to buttons so small targets stay easy to hit.

  • Magnetic cursor sticks to buttons
  • Pauses when you look away
  • Carries its own Python — installs nothing
  • Any ordinary webcam works

02 · how the tracking works

Three ideas keep it steady.

21 landmarks

Your hand as a skeleton

30 fps · MediaPipe

Every frame, a hand-tracking model returns 21 points — wrist, knuckles, fingertips. Gestures are just distances and angles between those points, so which fingers you raise or pinch decides what happens.

Arming

The engage circle

Open palm · 1 second

Hold up an open palm and a circle locks onto your hand — only then do you have the cursor. The circle scales itself to your distance from the camera, so a hand wandering into frame never grabs control by accident.

Attention gate

It knows when you look away

Face check · auto-pause

When you turn from the screen, AirMouse pauses; look back and control returns. Scratching your head or reaching for coffee doesn't click anything.

04 · download

Running in three steps.

  1. Download and extractOne 111 MB zip. Right-click → Extract All. Anywhere is fine.
  2. Double-click AirMouse.exeWindows shows “Windows protected your PC” → More info → Run anyway. Every unsigned app does this.
  3. Hold up an open palm for a secondThe circle locks onto your hand. You have the cursor.
