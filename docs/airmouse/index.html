Your webcam becomes your mouse.

Move your hand to move the cursor. Pinch to click. No extra hardware, and nothing gets installed on your machine.

Download for Windows Source

111 MB · delete the folder to uninstall

Getting started

  1. Download and extractRight-click the zip → Extract All. Anywhere is fine.
  2. Double-click AirMouse.exeWindows shows "Windows protected your PC" → More info → Run anyway. Every unsigned app does this.
  3. Hold up an open palm for a secondA circle locks onto your hand. You have the cursor.

Gestures

Do thisGet this
Open palm, 1sTake the cursor
Move your handMove the cursor
Thumb + indexClick · hold to drag
Thumb + middleRight click
Thumb + ring, moveVolume
Peace signScroll — farther = faster
Fist + swipeForward / Back
Fist + push downMinimise
1–4 fingers, other handLaunch an app
Lower your handLet go

Q quits · B opens settings · D shows live gesture numbers
