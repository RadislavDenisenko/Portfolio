AirMouse

Your webcam becomes your mouse. Move your hand to move the cursor. Pinch to click.

Download for Windows Source

111 MB · nothing to install · delete the folder to uninstall

Get it running

  1. Download the zipButton above. 111 MB.
  2. Right-click it → Extract AllAnywhere. Desktop is fine.
  3. Open the folder → double-click AirMouse.exeWindows says "Windows protected your PC" → click More info → Run anyway. That warning shows for any app without a paid certificate.

Then do this

  1. Hold up an open palm for 1 secondLike a high five. A circle locks on — you have the cursor.
  2. Move your handThe cursor follows.
  3. Touch thumb to index fingerThat's a click. Hold them together to drag.
  4. Press Q to quitB opens settings. D shows live gesture numbers.

Every gesture
