AirMouse

Your webcam becomes your mouse. Move your hand, pinch to click.

Download Source

111 MB · nothing to install · delete the folder to uninstall

Install

  1. Download & extractRight-click the zip → Extract All. Anywhere.
  2. Double-click AirMouse.exeWindows says "Windows protected your PC" → More info → Run anyway. Every unsigned app shows that.
  3. Hold up an open palm for 1 secondA circle locks on. You have the cursor.

Gestures

Do thisGet this
Open palm, 1sTake the cursor
Move your handMove the cursor
Thumb + indexClick · hold to drag
Thumb + middleRight click
Thumb + ring, moveVolume
Peace signScroll — farther = faster
Fist + swipeForward / Back
Fist + push downMinimise
1–4 fingers, other handLaunch an app
Lower your handLet go

Q quits · B opens settings · D shows live gesture numbers
