AirMouse

Downloadable v0.9.0 beta

A Windows app that turns any webcam into a touch-free mouse. Move your hand to move the cursor, pinch to click. No extra hardware.

Tracks 21 hand landmarks at 30 fps and drives the real cursor through the Win32 SendInput API. Gestures arm on finger count rather than how curled the hand is, so ordinary pointing can't accidentally fire navigation. A face-tracking attention gate pauses control when you look away from the screen. Packaged so it runs without installing Python.

Roomly

In progress

An apartment-first roommate finder. Swipe real listings; liking a place shows you the verified people who want it too — so finding a place and finding someone to share it with stop being two separate problems.

Matches rank on the things that actually cause roommate friction: budget, sleep schedule, cleanliness, pets, smoking. Identity verification gates access to matches, and messaging is restricted to mutual matches in the database itself rather than just hidden in the interface. Still building — not launched yet.
