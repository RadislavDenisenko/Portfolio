Roomly

An apartment-first roommate finder. You swipe real listings, and liking a place surfaces the verified people who want it too — so finding a place and finding someone to share it with stop being two separate problems. Compatibility ranks on budget, sleep schedule, cleanliness, pets and smoking. Identity verification gates access to matches, and messaging is restricted to mutual matches by Postgres row-level security rather than by the interface.
