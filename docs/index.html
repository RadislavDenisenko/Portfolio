AirMouse

Your hand is the mouse. A Windows app that turns any webcam into touch-free control — move the cursor in the air, pinch to click, swipe to switch windows. No extra hardware.

Tracks 21 hand landmarks at 30 fps and drives the real cursor through the Win32 SendInput API. Gestures arm on finger count so ordinary pointing can't misfire, and a face-tracking attention gate pauses control the moment you look away. Backed by 300+ automated tests and packaged to run without installing Python.

02 · Work in progress

Roomly

Finding an apartment and finding a roommate are the same problem — so why are they two different apps? Swipe real listings; liking a place surfaces the verified people who want it too.

Matches rank on what actually causes roommate friction: budget, sleep schedule, cleanliness, pets, smoking. Messaging opens only on mutual match, enforced in the database itself — not just hidden in the interface. First working draft built solo; currently being reworked, not launched yet.
