# reference/

Not shipped. Targets that agents compare their renders against.

- `hand-target.jpg` — the look the AirMouse hand must reach: a real hand,
  palm to camera, fingers spread, on deep navy, soft even studio light with a
  faint cool rim on the left edge. Note specifically: **fingernails**, the
  knuckle and joint creases, the palm crease lines, the slight redness at the
  fingertips and knuckles, the soft translucency at the thumb's edge, and how
  the forearm falls off into shadow. Open it and look before judging a render.
